## Testing the Bot

Once added to your server, test with:
- `.hycron` - Should display the help menu
- `.ngl [username] [duration]` - Start the NGL spam

## Notes

- The bot must be online (running) to respond to commands
- Make sure you have added your bot token to the hycron-bot.js file
- The bot needs permission to send messages and embeds in the channels where you use it
